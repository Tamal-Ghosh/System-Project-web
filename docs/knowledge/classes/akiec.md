# AKIEC — Actinic keratosis and intraepidermal carcinoma

Educational source-based paraphrase. Checked 2026-11-24. Not clinician-validated. General descriptions are not observed patient findings.

## AKIEC — Grouped label
AKIEC groups actinic keratosis with intraepidermal carcinoma. It does not resolve which entity is present. NCI addresses actinic keratosis alongside keratinocyte skin cancers.
Reference: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/hp/skin-treatment-pdq

## AKIEC — Assessment context
A clinician interprets the lesion with examination and relevant history; additional investigation depends on the findings. A class output cannot establish invasion or justify a procedure. NCI PDQ is evidence information from the United States, not a patient-specific treatment instruction.
Reference: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/hp/skin-treatment-pdq
