# BCC — Basal cell carcinoma

Educational source-based paraphrase. Checked 2026-11-24. Not clinician-validated. General descriptions are not observed patient findings.

## BCC — Overview
Basal cell carcinoma is a skin cancer that usually grows slowly and seldom spreads to distant sites. It can still invade nearby tissue. A classifier label alone does not confirm that this patient has BCC.
Reference: https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/basal-cell-carcinoma

## BCC — Presentation and assessment
The diagnosis is assessed by a dermatologist. Clinical appearance, examination and, where appropriate, tissue sampling inform assessment. Typical descriptions in educational material must not be reported as observed findings in an uploaded image without corresponding evidence.
Reference: https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/basal-cell-carcinoma

## BCC — Management context
Management is individualized. Relevant considerations include depth, anatomical location, general health and whether a lesion is recurrent. Surgery, including selected use of Mohs surgery, is among treatment approaches; other approaches depend on the clinical situation. A prediction score cannot select a treatment or establish stage. This AAD patient reference describes US dermatology practice, not a local prescribing protocol.
Reference: https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer/basal-cell-carcinoma
