Educational source-based paraphrase. Checked 2026-11-24. Not clinician-validated. General descriptions are not observed patient findings.

## DF — Overview and presentation
Dermatofibroma is a common benign fibrous skin lesion. It is often a firm papule or nodule, commonly on a limb. Pinching can cause a dimple, but this is an examination finding and cannot be inferred from a model score.
Reference: https://dermnetnz.org/topics/dermatofibroma

## DF — Assessment context
Many dermatofibromas can be recognized clinically. An atypical or uncertain lesion may need further evaluation. A classifier cannot demonstrate firmness, a dimple sign or benignity. DermNet is an educational dermatology reference rather than a local prescribing guideline.
Reference: https://dermnetnz.org/topics/dermatofibroma
