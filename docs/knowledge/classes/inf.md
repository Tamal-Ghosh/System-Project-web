## INF — Grouped category
INF combines inflammatory and infectious conditions. A model result cannot identify an organism, distinguish all inflammatory diseases or select an antimicrobial.

## INF — Interpretation
Clinical symptoms, duration, examination and other indicated assessments may be needed to determine the underlying condition. There is no single presentation or treatment applicable to this entire class. This benchmark taxonomy is not a clinical management guideline.
