# MAL_OTH — Other malignant proliferations

Educational source-based paraphrase. Checked 2026-11-24. Not clinician-validated. General descriptions are not observed patient findings.

## MAL_OTH — Grouped category
MAL_OTH is the benchmark group for other malignant proliferations, including collision lesions, separate from the named melanoma, BCC and SCCKA groups.
Reference: https://challenge.isic-archive.com/landing/milk10k/

## MAL_OTH — Interpretation
This class does not resolve histological subtype, stage or prognosis. Do not assign generic disease features or a treatment to the whole category. Clinical and pathological clarification depend on the actual lesion; taxonomy alone cannot specify the next procedure.
Reference: https://challenge.isic-archive.com/landing/milk10k/
