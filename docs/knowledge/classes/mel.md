## MEL — Overview
Melanoma is cancer arising in melanocytes, the pigment-producing cells. It can develop in skin in different body locations. A change in the appearance of a mole or pigmented area can be a reason for clinical evaluation.
Reference: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/patient/melanoma-treatment-pdq

## MEL — Assessment and interpretation
Clinical history and skin examination are part of diagnosis. Confirmation and further assessment may involve biopsy. Pathology and staging guide management; a classifier score does not establish either. General descriptions of changing moles do not demonstrate that such a change occurred in the current image. This NCI patient reference is US educational information, not an individualized management recommendation.
