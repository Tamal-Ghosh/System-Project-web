# NV — Melanocytic nevus

Educational source-based paraphrase. Checked 2026-11-24. Not clinician-validated. General descriptions are not observed patient findings.

## NV — Overview
A melanocytic nevus, or mole, is a benign proliferation of melanocytes. Moles can differ in colour, size and clinical form. Some are present early in life and others develop later.
Reference: https://dermnetnz.org/topics/melanocytic-naevus

## NV — Assessment context
Clinical context and examination are important when a lesion is changing or atypical. Some lesions can resemble melanoma. An NV score cannot establish benignity or rule out malignancy, and a single image cannot establish change over time. DermNet supplies educational reference information, not a prediction of outcome.
Reference: https://dermnetnz.org/topics/melanocytic-naevus
