## SCCKA — Grouped label
The benchmark groups squamous cell carcinoma and keratoacanthoma as SCCKA. NCI discusses squamous cell carcinoma separately from basal cell carcinoma and melanoma. The grouped prediction does not determine histological subtype.
Reference: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/hp/skin-treatment-pdq

## SCCKA — Assessment context
Assessment and treatment depend on clinical and pathological evaluation. A model output cannot determine depth, stage or management. NCI PDQ summarizes evidence in a US context; it is not a prescription or automatic clinical decision rule.
Reference: https://www.cancer.gov/types/skin/hp/skin-treatment-pdq
