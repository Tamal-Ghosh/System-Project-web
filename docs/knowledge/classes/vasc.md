# VASC — Vascular lesions

Educational source-based paraphrase. Checked 2026-11-24. Not clinician-validated. General descriptions are not observed patient findings.

## VASC — Grouped category
VASC denotes a group of vascular lesions in the benchmark. It is not a single specific vascular diagnosis. A score does not identify a subtype.
Reference: https://challenge.isic-archive.com/landing/milk10k/

## VASC — Interpretation
Do not assume one cause, natural history or treatment for all vascular lesions. Explain the broad label and the need to interpret it using clinical context. This research taxonomy supplies label definitions rather than medical management guidance.
Reference: https://challenge.isic-archive.com/landing/milk10k/
